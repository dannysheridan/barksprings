### **Car Rentals**

Highly recommended — rideshares takes **15–20 minutes** to show up. Austin airport has on-site rental car center, it’s a fairly painless process. 

### **Nearest Stores**

- Grocery - H-E-B is 10 min away, it has everything
- Home Depot and Walgreens are 10 min away in Dripping Springs

### **Restaurants**

Casual

- Salt Lick for BBQ
- Crepe Crazy or lunch
- Pieous for lunch for dinner

Fancy

- Tillies at Camp Lucy (20 min), make a reservation
- Whiskey Ridge (20 min), sit outside on the back, covered patio

**Activities**

Active

- Pickleball at Pickle Y'all. Open play on Saturday mornings.
- Tennis, Pickleball, or Padel at Dripping Springs Racquet Club
  - Rackets, paddles, and balls are located in the entryway cabinets
- Hiking
  - Reimers Ranch (20 min) has water, wildflowers, and rolling hills
  - Zilker Park and Barton Creek Greenbelt (30min, downtown Austin) are popular areas and have water features and lots of shade on the trails
  - Perdenales State Park (30 min) need to make a reservation, outstanding trails
- Horseback Riding
  - Trail ride at Southern Trails near Canyon Lake, book [here](https://www.southerntrailrides.com/) in advance!
- Rock Climbing
  - Schedule 2+ weeks ahead with Sam Brodnax at Texas Climbing Adventures, at Reimers Ranch, great experience. [info@texasclimbingadventures.com](mailto:info@texasclimbingadventures.com) or
  -

Drinking

- Driftwood Estate Winery (5 min), good vibes, no kids or dogs allowed, average wine, great vibes, food on the weekends, good board game tables
- Devil's Backbone Tavern (20 min), dive bar with [live music](https://www.devilsbackbonetavern.com/live-music), cool vibes
- Suds Monkey Brewery (20 min), kid-friendly, beer and pizza
- Equipment Room (40 min), in downtown Austin, cocktail bar with funky music, make a reservation [here](https://resy.com/cities/austin-tx/venues/equipment-room?date=2026-05-26\&seats=2)
